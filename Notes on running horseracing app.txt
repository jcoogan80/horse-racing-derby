A few things to keep in mind going forward:

The CAPTCHA may not show every time — once Equibase recognizes your browser session it sometimes lets you through without it. So don't be surprised if future runs go straight through without needing the 30 second wait.
To add more dates just use the GUI — open HorseRacingGUI.py and pick dates from the calendar. Everything flows through automatically.
To add other tracks just change AQU to whatever track code you want in the GUI dropdown — it will automatically create a new tab in Google Sheets for that track.
